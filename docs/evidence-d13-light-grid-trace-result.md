# D13 tiled-light usage trace: result

## Result

D13 is visually unchanged: the moving square-grid artifact remains on the
main-menu aircraft and the floor around it. The trace does not expose a missing
clear, transition, or UAV dependency on the named tiled-light resources.

It does expose a stable relationship between the tiled-light, self-light, and
reflection stages. `rtLightRefs` is placed in UAV state around a repeatable
compute sequence;
`rtSelfLight` is cleared and rendered as a full-resolution scene target; and
the first draws after `rtSelfLight` becomes shader-readable use the same two
pixel shaders that D12 identified on `m_prtTargetReflections`. The latter is a
strong ordering correlation, not proof of the exact descriptor read, because
D13 intentionally records nearby read candidates rather than resolving the
complete descriptor table.

## Stable producer and consumer candidates

The normal `rtLightRefs27` cycle contains:

1. transition from shader-read state (`0xc0`) to UAV (`0x8`);
2. compute shader `ce5553a11c1e3c3d`, groups `10,5,1`;
3. self-light clear and scene rendering;
4. compute shader `e41c75bf472dc42b`, groups `10,5,1`;
5. an explicit resource UAV barrier;
6. compute shader `14096b77d9f7cb60`, groups `10,5,2`;
7. another explicit resource UAV barrier;
8. compute shader `651194bd0a21772e`, groups `10,5,1`, followed by further
   explicit UAV barriers;
9. transition of both `rtLightRefs` and `rtSelfLight` to shader-read state;
10. two nearby draws with vertex shader `7ab2e6bc4f546a86` and pixel shaders
    `df0bd777fd1bb89d` and `a2d104d5c813322e`; and
11. command-list submission.

D12 identified those same vertex/pixel shader pairs as writers of
`m_prtTargetReflections`. This narrows the interaction to a real, repeatable
part of the frame graph. Descriptor-resolution instrumentation or a frame
capture is still required to prove which exact self-light/light-list view each
reflection draw reads.

## Decision

The broad form of G17 is weakened: D13 finds neither an uncleared per-frame
`rtSelfLight` target nor a missing transition/UAV barrier around `rtLightRefs`.
It does not validate the values computed by the four light-list shaders,
descriptor/view selection for the 3D `R32_UINT` resource, or the reflection
shader's interpretation of that data.

The next passive discriminator is to dump and inspect the exact DXIL and SPIR-V
for the stable hashes. This can establish thread-group coverage, resource/view
types, array/3D addressing, and translation differences without changing game
behavior. A one-setting visual A/B may follow, but only if it cleanly removes
one stage; the installed `startup.cfg` exposes no direct SSR/reflection toggle.
No Proton quirk or application override is justified by D13.
